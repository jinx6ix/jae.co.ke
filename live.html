Frequently Asked Questions About Wheelchair Accessible Safari Kenya

Do you offer wheelchair accessible safari vehicles?

Yes — we operate Kenya's only fleet of custom 4x4 Land Cruisers with German hydraulic lifts, pop-up roofs, and medical-grade wheelchair securement rated to 400kg. Every vehicle carries climate control, panoramic windows, and an onboard medical kit, and our drivers are trained in wheelchair transfer and securement before every departure.

Can I rent a wheelchair accessible safari vehicle?

Yes. We offer private hire of our fully adapted safari vehicles with experienced drivers, which works well for independent travellers or small private groups. The daily rate covers the vehicle, driver, fuel, and park-area driving. A professional driver-guide, camping equipment, or a satellite phone can be added as extras.

What destinations do you cover?

We operate in Kenya, Tanzania, Rwanda, and Uganda, including the Masai Mara, Amboseli, Tsavo, the Serengeti, Volcanoes National Park, and Bwindi. Every destination we quote has been checked for accessible vehicle access and step-free accommodation. If a specific park or lodge cannot meet your mobility needs, we say so before you book.

How much does a wheelchair accessible safari in Kenya cost in 2026?

Prices start from $4,850 USD for a 7-day luxury accessible safari package. A custom quote includes the hydraulic lift vehicle, disability-friendly lodges, all game drives, park entry fees, and meals. Longer routes, private vehicle hire, and upgraded lodge categories are priced separately, and we always send a written quotation before any deposit is taken.

Can I stay in my own wheelchair during game drives?

Yes. Our German hydraulic lift vehicles are designed so you can remain in your own wheelchair for the entire game drive, with 400kg capacity securement systems anchoring the chair to the cabin floor. The lift is operated by a trained driver, and the pop-up roof means you get an unobstructed view without leaving your chair at any point.

Is gorilla trekking accessible for wheelchair users in Rwanda?

Yes. We arrange adapted vehicle transfers to Volcanoes National Park, wheelchair-accessible viewing platforms at the trekking sites, and lodges near the park built with barrier-free access. Trail surfaces vary, so we assess your specific requirements and match the route, guide, and group size to what the terrain actually allows that day.

What makes your wheelchair accessible safari vehicles the best in Kenya?

German hydraulic lifts rated to 400kg, four-point medical-grade restraints, panoramic windows, climate control, and onboard medical kits. Every vehicle is maintained on a dedicated lift-service schedule, and every driver completes securement and transfer training before being assigned to an accessible departure.

Are your lodges wheelchair accessible?

All partner camps and lodges are audited for roll-in showers, ramps, widened doorways, and trained support staff, so barrier-free access is the standard rather than an upgrade. We confirm the specific room type and bathroom layout for your party when quoting, and flag any camp where a particular feature is unavailable before you commit.
